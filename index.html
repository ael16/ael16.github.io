Bio

Alvin Lirio is a physician-scientist with a PhD in genetic epidemiology. His work centres on genome-wide association studies (GWAS) and downstream analyses in large biobanks and population cohorts, bringing together genomic, clinical and lifestyle data to find disease mechanisms and potential therapeutic targets.

He trained as a doctor in the Philippines and practised as a general physician before moving into research. His PhD at the University of Leicester examined the genetic and non-genetic determinants of respiratory traits across the life course, using UK Biobank, the EXCEED cohort and the Cebu Longitudinal Health and Nutrition Survey. He now works on the genetics of ocular traits at the Ulverscroft Eye Unit.

Grounded in clinical practice and work with Filipino cohorts, he has a long-standing commitment to improving genomic representation of underrepresented populations.

Research interests
  • Respiratory and cardiometabolic population genetics
  • Longitudinal disease trajectories and gene-by-environment interactions (smoking, diet, lifestyle, medications)
  • Rare variant and non-coding variant analyses
  • Multi-ancestry genomics and underrepresented populations

Research and scholarship

Longitudinal lung function decline in COPD

A GWAS of FEV1 decline in people with COPD in UK Biobank, using linked electronic health records and conditional linear mixed models to capture lung function trajectories.

Manuscript under review

Preserved ratio impaired spirometry (PRISm)

A genome-wide association study of PRISm, drawing on the EXCEED cohort, as part of his PhD.

European Respiratory Journal, 2023

Asthma genetics in a Filipino cohort

A candidate-variant association study of self-reported asthma in mothers of the Cebu Longitudinal Health and Nutrition Survey, one of Southeast Asia's longest-running population cohorts.

BMC Genomic Data, 2026

Genetics of the fovea and photoreceptors

Genome-scale analyses of retinal traits in UK Biobank, including foveal development and a GWAS of sub-foveal outer segment thickness in about 54,000 participants using OCT-derived phenotypes.

IOVS 2025 · medRxiv 2026 · UK-EGG 2026

Type 2 diabetes in Filipinos

A UCSF collaboration at the Institute of Human Genetics, NIH Philippines, on genetic, epigenetic and metagenomic determinants of type 2 diabetes.

Human Molecular Genetics, 2019
Methods and tools

GWAS and post-GWAS analysis, fine-mapping, LD-score regression, genetic risk scores, functional mapping, linear and logistic regression, conditional linear mixed models. R, PLINK, REGENIE, STATA, Linux and HPC environments. UK Biobank, EXCEED, CLHNS and electronic health records.
